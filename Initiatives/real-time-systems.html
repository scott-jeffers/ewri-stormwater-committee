Real-Time Systems

Advancing real-time monitoring and control systems for stormwater management and flood mitigation using cutting-edge sensor technology and data analytics.

Overview

Real-time control (RTC) systems are transforming how municipalities manage stormwater infrastructure. This initiative explores best practices for integrating models with sensors, forecasts, and control logic to optimize system performance during storm events.

We are developing guidance on model integration, data assimilation, forecasting methods, and control strategies that practitioners can apply to green infrastructure, detention systems, and conveyance networks.

Recent Updates

February 2026

Initiative formed. Committee is identifying case studies and practitioners with RTC implementation experience to inform guidance development.
