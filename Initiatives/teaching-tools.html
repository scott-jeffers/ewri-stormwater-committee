Teaching Tools

Developing comprehensive educational resources and interactive tools to support stormwater modeling instruction and professional training programs.

Overview

The Teaching Tools initiative focuses on creating high-quality educational materials that help instructors, students, and practitioners learn and teach stormwater modeling effectively. This includes curriculum guides, example models, video tutorials, and interactive learning modules.

Our goal is to lower the barrier to entry for new modelers while providing advanced resources for experienced professionals looking to stay current with evolving methodologies.

Recent Updates

February 2026

Initiative launched. Committee is gathering input on priority teaching resources and identifying existing materials to curate and enhance.

Key Milestones

  • Initiative approved by committee
  • Needs assessment and resource inventory
  • Develop curriculum outline
  • Pilot teaching materials

How to Contribute

We welcome contributions from educators and practitioners. Share your teaching materials, suggest topics for new resources, or volunteer to develop content. Contact the committee to get involved.
